It's easy to add an auto-updating, colour-coded, read-only view of a public calendar without manually copying events or polluting your own calendar.

<u>TL;DR generic instructions:</u>

1. find an option to **Subscribe**/**Add calendar from URL**/**New Calendar Subscription**/**Add Subscribed Calendar**
2. paste the **URL**

<u>Specific instructions for popular apps:</u>

## Google

1. open *Google Calendar* in a browser (**not mobile**)
2. find **Other calendars** in the left sidebar and click **+**
3. choose **From URL**
4. paste the URL and click **Add calendar**

## Outlook

1. open *Outlook Calendar*
2. click **Add calendar** and **Subscribe from web**
3. paste the URL, give it a name, and click **Import**

## Apple

### Mac

1. **File** and **New Calendar Subscription**
2. paste the URL and click **Subscribe**

### iPhone/iPad

1. **Settings** -> **Calendar**
2. **Accounts** -> **Add Account** -> **Other** -> **Add Subscribed Calendar**
3. paste the URL
